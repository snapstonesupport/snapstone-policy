Privacy Policy

SnapStone · Effective 11 November 2026

1. About this policy

SnapStone (“SnapStone”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a social app where you can share photos, videos and stories, follow people, chat, and make voice and video calls. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using SnapStone you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your name, username, bio and profile photo.
  • The mobile number and/or Gmail address you use to sign up or log in, including when you sign in with Google or verify with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Your password. It is handled by our sign-in provider (Google Firebase Authentication) and we cannot see it in readable form.

Content you create and share

  • Posts (photos and videos), captions, music tags, comments, likes, saved posts and stories.
  • Messages you send, including text, photos, videos, replies and reactions.
  • Reports and support tickets you send to us.

Activity and settings

  • Who you follow, who follows you, accounts you block, and your follow requests.
  • Your settings such as private account, online status, notification choices, language and dark mode.

Camera, microphone and notifications

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo or video, use filters, or make a video call.
  • Microphone: used only when you record a video or make a voice or video call.
  • Face and hand filters work on your device. We do not store or send face or hand data to our servers.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, we store a push notification token that identifies your device so we can send you alerts.

Technical information

  • Like most online services, our service providers (listed below) can see your IP address and basic connection details when your device connects to them.
  • Some settings and cached data are saved on your own device (local storage) so the app works faster.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account, and verify that it is you.
  • Show your posts, stories and profile to other people according to your privacy settings.
  • Deliver messages, calls and notifications.
  • Respond to support requests and reports, keep the community safe, and prevent spam, fraud and abuse.
  • Fix problems and improve the app.
  • Follow the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who can see your information

  • Public account: your profile, posts and stories can be seen by anyone using SnapStone.
  • Private account: only followers you approve can see your posts and stories.
  • Messages are visible to you and the person or people you send them to.
  • Stories are visible to others for 24 hours. You can still see your own old stories in Story history.
  • If you report a post, account or chat, our support team may review the reported content and the related conversation.

5. Calls

Voice and video calls are sent directly between the devices of the people on the call whenever possible. The call is encrypted while it travels, and we do not record calls. If a direct connection is not possible, the call may pass through a relay server, which can see technical details such as IP addresses but not the content of the call.

6. Service providers we use

To run SnapStone we use the following providers. They process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in (Authentication), our database (Realtime Database) and push notifications (Cloud Messaging).
  • Cloudinary: storing and delivering the photos and videos you upload.
  • Cloudflare Workers: sending push notifications.
  • Google STUN servers and Open Relay (Metered) TURN servers: connecting calls.
  • Google (MediaPipe models) and jsDelivr: loading the filter models and app libraries that run on your device.

SnapStone does not currently show third-party ads and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. We may share information if required by law, court order or to protect the safety of users and the public.

7. Where your data is stored

Our providers may store and process data on servers outside your country, including outside India. We choose providers that use reasonable security measures.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete a post, story or message, it is removed from the app. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your account data within 30 days, except where we must keep some information to follow the law, resolve disputes or prevent abuse.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, username, bio and photo from Edit profile.
  • Make your account private, hide your online status, block accounts and choose which notifications you get from Settings.
  • Delete your own posts, stories and messages.
  • Turn off camera, microphone and notification access anytime in your phone settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or delete your account, by writing to snapstonesupport@gmail.com from your registered email or mobile number details.

10. Security

We use industry-standard services and encrypted connections to protect your data. No app or internet service is completely secure, so please keep your login details private and log out on shared devices.

11. Children

SnapStone is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18, please use SnapStone only with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make important changes, we will update the date at the top. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact us

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy? Email snapstonesupport@gmail.com or use Support inside the app (Settings → Support).
